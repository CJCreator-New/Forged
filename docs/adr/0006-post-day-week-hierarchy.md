# Posts belong to a Day, and Days belong to a Week, which owns one Brief and one Pillar

Status: accepted

A Post belongs to one Day for one Channel. A Week holds seven Days built from one Brief and one Pillar draft, and each Day's Posts are adapted from that Pillar. We chose this over standalone posts because week mode depends on one shared Pillar, and changing the Pillar regenerates the adapted posts. A Deck belongs to its Post, not to the Week.
